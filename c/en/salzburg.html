🎼 Salzburg

The 'Salt Fortress' where Mozart was born and The Sound of Music came to life

📖 The Story

Long ago in the mountains of Austria, there was a town so rich in salt that people called it 'white gold.' The town's very name, Salzburg, means 'Salt Fortress'—and salt built almost everything you see there today. Barges piled high with salt blocks floated down the green Salzach River, making merchants and rulers wealthy for hundreds of years.

To protect all that treasure, the prince-archbishops who ruled Salzburg built a giant fortress on the hill above the town. It's called Hohensalzburg, and it's one of the biggest castles left standing in all of Europe. For 900 years, no enemy army ever managed to break through its walls.

In 1756, in a cozy yellow house on a street called Getreidegasse, a baby was born who would grow up to be one of the greatest musicians ever: Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart. He was so talented that he was already composing his own little pieces of music by the time he was five years old, and soon he was playing piano for kings and queens.

Two hundred years later, Salzburg became famous all over again—this time as the real-life backdrop for the movie The Sound of Music. Kids twirled through its flower-filled gardens on camera, and today music still fills the streets every summer during the city's giant festival. With its golden domes and mountain views, people even call Salzburg 'the Rome of the North.'

📍 Places to visit

📍 Hohensalzburg Fortress

This giant fortress has watched over Salzburg from its hilltop for over 900 years and is one of the best-preserved medieval castles in Europe. You can ride a cable car straight up to it and peek out at the whole city and mountains below. No enemy ever managed to conquer it by force.

📍 Mozart's Birthplace

A bright yellow house on a lively shopping street where baby Mozart was born in 1756. Inside you can see his tiny childhood violin and the very piano he once played. It's amazing to think such a small house held such a big talent!

📍 Mirabell Palace and Gardens

Beautiful gardens full of flowers, fountains, and stone statues, built by a prince-archbishop for someone he loved. This is where the kids in The Sound of Music skipped and sang past the little gnome statues. On a clear day, you can even see Hohensalzburg Fortress peeking over the treetops!

📍 Salzburg Cathedral

A grand baroque cathedral right in the heart of the Old Town, with a dome so huge it can be seen from almost anywhere in the city. Mozart was baptized here as a baby and later played the organ inside. Step through its bronze doors and look up—the painted ceiling feels like it goes on forever.
