🌃 Shanghai

The city where old European buildings and a futuristic skyline stare at each other across a river

📖 The Story

Long, long ago, where Shanghai now stands, there was just a small fishing village where the Huangpu River meets the sea. Fishermen mended their nets, and boats bobbed gently on the muddy water. Nobody dreamed this quiet little spot would one day become one of the biggest cities on Earth.

Everything changed about 180 years ago, when Shanghai's port opened up to trading ships from all over the world. Foreign merchants built grand stone banks and trading houses along the riverbank, a strip now called the Bund. Ships loaded with tea, silk, and spices came and went day and night, and the small village grew into a bustling international harbor.

Shanghai kept growing and growing, but it never stopped surprising people. Across the river, where there had only ever been farmland, people dreamed up a brand-new district called Pudong. Almost overnight, in city-growing time, shining skyscrapers rose into the sky, including a giant pearl-shaped tower and a twisting glass spire.

Today, if you stand on the Bund and look across the Huangpu River, you can see two totally different worlds at once. On one side stand elegant old stone buildings from over a hundred years ago; on the other, a shining forest of futuristic towers. That's why people say Shanghai is a city where the past and the future stare at each other across the water.

🤩 Did you know?

  • 1Shanghai Tower's glass skin twists 120 degrees as it rises—it's one of the tallest buildings on Earth.
  • 2The Maglev train from Shanghai's airport floats on magnets instead of wheels and zooms over 430 km/h!
  • 3The Bund has more than 50 buildings, each built in a totally different style—walking along it feels like visiting many countries at once.
  • 4The bridge in Yu Garden zigzags back and forth. People once believed evil spirits could only walk in straight lines, so the zigzag kept them out!
  • 5Shanghai's port is one of the busiest in the world—an enormous number of shipping containers pass through every single year.
  • 6The name 'Shanghai' once simply meant 'up from the sea,' because the city grew right where a river meets the ocean.

📍 Places to visit

📍 The Bund

A riverside promenade lined with grand European-style buildings from a hundred years ago. Look across the water and you'll spot the shining skyscrapers of futuristic Pudong.

📍 Yu Garden

A beautiful garden built back in the Ming dynasty. Cross its zigzag bridge and you'll find a pond full of swimming koi fish.

📍 Oriental Pearl Tower

A pink, pearl-shaped tower with a glass-floored observation deck. Stand on it and you can look straight down at the city below your feet!

📍 Shanghai Tower

A twisting tower that spirals high into the sky. Ride the world's fastest elevator and you'll reach the top in a flash.
