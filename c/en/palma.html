🏖️ Palma de Mallorca

The island where a golden cathedral glows by the sea and a dragon guards a hidden lake

📖 The Story

Long before Spain even existed, mysterious islanders were already living on Mallorca. Thousands of years ago, they built strange, giant stone towers called talaiots to guard their villages. Nobody wrote down why — the towers just stood there, silent and huge, for thousands and thousands of years.

Many sailors came after them — Romans, then Moors who built palaces by the sea. Then one day in the year 1229, a king named Jaume I sailed in with a huge fleet of ships and took over the island. Legend says he promised that if his ships landed safely, he would build the grandest cathedral anyone had ever seen.

He kept his promise. The cathedral called La Seu took almost 400 years to finish, perched right on a cliff above the sea so that ships sailing by could see it glowing like a golden crown. Inside, one of the biggest round windows in the world lets in so much light that twice a year, sunbeams paint a giant glowing figure-eight on the stone wall.

But Mallorca keeps another secret underground. Deep in a hidden cave, there's a lake so calm and so dark that musicians float across it in little rowboats, playing music that echoes through the rock. Down there, no sunlight has ever shone — only lanterns, water, and songs.

🤩 Did you know?

  • 1The ancient stone towers called talaiots are over 3,000 years old — older than the Colosseum in Rome!
  • 2La Seu Cathedral's round window is so big, you could almost fit a whole house inside its circle.
  • 3Twice a year, around February 2 and November 11, sunlight beams through that window and paints a glowing figure-eight on the wall — crowds gather just to see it.
  • 4Inside the Caves of Drach is Lake Martel, one of the largest underground lakes on Earth, first explored by a diver in 1896.
  • 5Every single day, hidden musicians float across that underground lake in rowboats, giving a live concert in total darkness.
  • 6The composer Chopin once spent a rainy winter on Mallorca in an old mountain monastery, writing some of his most famous piano music there.

📍 Places to visit

📍 La Seu Cathedral

Poking up right above the sea, this cathedral took almost 400 years to build. Look for its giant round window — twice a year, sunlight through it paints a glowing figure-eight right on the stone wall.

📍 Caves of Drach

Deep underground near Porto Cristo hides Lake Martel, one of the biggest underground lakes on Earth. Musicians row across it in little boats, playing music that echoes through the dark stone halls.

📍 Bellver Castle

This round castle sits on a hill overlooking Palma's bay — one of the few perfectly circular castles in all of Europe. Kids can imagine knights watching for ships from its tall towers.

📍 Es Trenc Beach

With powder-white sand and water so clear and turquoise it looks painted on, this beach makes people say "wait, are we in the Caribbean?" It has stayed wild and undeveloped, just sand, sea, and sky.
