🎡 Shenzhen

The fishing village that grew into a giant city in just 40 years

📖 The Story

Not so long ago, there was a quiet little fishing village called Shenzhen, tucked next to the sea in southern China. Only about 30,000 people lived there. Farmers grew rice in green fields, and fishing boats bobbed along the shore. Just across the water sat a much bigger, busier city called Hong Kong.

Then, in 1980, something amazing happened. China chose this sleepy village to try a brand-new idea first—before anywhere else in the whole country. Shenzhen became China's very first 'Special Economic Zone,' a place where new kinds of factories, shops, and businesses could grow fast and free.

And grow it did—faster than almost any city in human history! Rice paddies turned into busy streets. Small houses turned into shining skyscrapers. In just a few decades, that tiny fishing village of 30,000 became a giant city of more than ten million people, buzzing with inventors and tech companies.

Today Shenzhen is famous for more than skyscrapers—it's home to two dazzling theme parks. At Window of the World you can stand beside a mini Eiffel Tower and tiny Egyptian pyramids all in one afternoon. And at Happy Valley, roller coasters race through the sky. Not bad for a village that used to just fish!

📍 Places to visit

📍 Window of the World

A park packed with miniature versions of the world's most famous landmarks—the Eiffel Tower, the pyramids, even Niagara Falls. You can 'visit' the whole world in a single afternoon!

📍 Happy Valley Shenzhen

A thrilling theme park full of roller coasters and lively shows. It's the reason Shenzhen is famous for fun as much as for skyscrapers.

📍 Shenzhen Bay Park

A long waterfront park with a view of Hong Kong just across the bay. At sunset, the city's twinkling lights spill across the water.

📍 Ping An Finance Centre

A gleaming super-tall skyscraper standing where rice paddies once grew. It shows, in one glance, just how fast and how high Shenzhen has grown.
