🍵 Hangzhou

The lake so beautiful, people call it 'heaven on earth'

📖 The Story

Long, long ago, Chinese people came up with a saying: 'Above, there is heaven. Below, there are Suzhou and Hangzhou.' Hangzhou earned that huge compliment because of one dazzling lake right in its center, called West Lake. About 800 years ago, Hangzhou was even the capital of an entire kingdom, and a famous traveler named Marco Polo later called it 'the finest and most splendid city in the world.'

West Lake also holds one of China's most magical legends. People say a white snake spirit fell in love with a young man and turned herself into a beautiful woman to be with him. A monk who thought she was dangerous trapped her forever beneath a tower by the lake called Leifeng Pagoda—and kids in China still love telling that bittersweet tale today.

Just outside the city, misty green hills grow one of the world's most famous teas, called Longjing, or 'Dragon Well' tea. Long ago, an emperor loved the tea bushes there so much that he picked the leaves with his own hands—and some of those very same bushes are still growing today! Every spring, farmers still pick the tender young leaves one by one, by hand.

Today, West Lake looks almost like an ancient painting come to life. Weeping willows lean over arched stone bridges, little boats drift past pink lotus flowers, and long causeways built centuries ago still let people stroll right across the water. Stand there at sunset, and you'll understand exactly why people have called this place 'paradise on Earth' for a thousand years.

📍 Places to visit

📍 West Lake

The shimmering lake at the very heart of Hangzhou, ringed by hills, bridges, and gardens. Poets have written about it for a thousand years, and it's the very place where the white snake legend is said to have unfolded.

📍 Leifeng Pagoda

This tower by the lake is where the legendary white snake spirit was said to be trapped forever. The original pagoda actually collapsed in 1924, but it was rebuilt—and you can even ride an elevator up for a sweeping view of the whole lake.

📍 Lingyin Temple

One of China's oldest and most famous Buddhist temples, tucked among misty forested hills for over 1,700 years. Right next to it stands a limestone peak carved with hundreds of smiling stone Buddhas, called 'the Peak That Flew Here.'

📍 Longjing Tea Village

Rolling green tea terraces just outside the city, home of the famous Longjing ('Dragon Well') tea. Walk among the neat rows of bushes, and you might spot farmers hand-picking the tender young leaves, just as they've done for centuries.
