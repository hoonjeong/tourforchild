🦘 Perth

The wonderfully faraway, sunshine-soaked city that once lit up the night for an astronaut in space

📖 The Story

On the wild west coast of Australia sits a sunny city called Perth, wrapped around a sparkling river. It's often called one of the most far-away big cities on the whole planet — the next major city, Adelaide, is over 2,000 kilometers away, further than crossing several countries! That means Perth feels like its own little world, with golden beaches on one side and red desert on the other.

Long before Perth existed, a Dutch explorer named Willem de Vlamingh sailed up the river in 1697. He and his sailors nearly fell over in shock — the swans gliding on the water were jet black! Back then, everyone in Europe believed all swans were white, so black swans seemed almost magical. De Vlamingh named the river the Swan River, and centuries later, the city that grew on its banks took the swan as its symbol.

The very same explorer then sailed to a small island nearby and spotted funny little creatures hopping through the bushes. He was so sure they were giant rats that he named the island 'Rotte nest' — rat's nest! It took a long time for people to realize these weren't rats at all, but cheerful, smiling marsupials called quokkas, now famous the world over for hopping right up to visitors for a selfie.

Perth has one more surprise up its sleeve. In 1962, astronaut John Glenn was zooming over Australia in space when the people of Perth switched on every single light they had, just so he could see their city glowing far below. He was amazed, and ever since, Perth has been nicknamed the 'City of Light' — a whole city bright enough to wave hello from space!

📍 Places to visit

📍 Kings Park

One of the world's largest inner-city parks, perched on a hill above the Swan River. From here you can look down at the very water where black swans once amazed an explorer, with the whole shining city spread out behind it.

📍 Rottnest Island

A short ferry ride from Perth, this island is home to thousands of quokkas — the very animals a Dutch explorer once mistook for giant rats. Hop off the boat and you might have one hopping right up to you for a selfie!

📍 Fremantle

A historic port town where old ships once arrived after the long, lonely voyage across the ocean. Its convict-built prison, now a World Heritage site, is full of true tales of daring escapes.

📍 Elizabeth Quay

A sparkling waterfront right on the Swan River, where dolphins sometimes swim by and the whole city lights up at night. Stand here after dark and imagine every window glowing, just like the night Perth said hello to space.
