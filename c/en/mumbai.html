🎬 Mumbai

Seven islands stitched into one movie-loving city by the sea

📖 The Story

Long ago, seven little islands lay scattered along the western coast of India. Shallow seawater and muddy flats separated them from one another. But bit by bit, people began filling in the gaps with earth and stone. Over hundreds of years, the islands joined together one by one, until at last they became one great stretch of land. That land is the city we now call Mumbai!

Because of this stitching-together, Mumbai grew a long, winding coastline that curls along the sea. Right at its edge stands a giant stone archway called the Gateway of India. Built about a hundred years ago, it was the very first thing sailors saw as their ships arrived from across the ocean. Today, it's still Mumbai's most famous face.

Mumbai has another claim to fame that the whole world knows: Bollywood, its dazzling film town. Here, more singing, dancing, colorful movies are made every year than in any other film industry on Earth. When it comes to the sheer number of films made in a year, Mumbai wins first place!

Mumbai is also home to some very clever delivery workers called dabbawalas. Every morning they pick up warm, home-cooked lunchboxes and carry them by bicycle, train, and handcart, delivering each one to the exact right office desk by lunchtime. Instead of written addresses, they use colors and symbols to find their way—and they almost never make a mistake, which is why schools around the world study how they do it. A city built by joining islands together is still singing, delivering, and buzzing with life today!

📍 Places to visit

📍 Gateway of India

A giant stone arch facing out to sea. Built about a hundred years ago, it was the very first sight that greeted people arriving by ship. It's still the picture that comes to mind first when you think of Mumbai.

📍 Marine Drive

A long, bow-shaped road curving beside the Arabian Sea. At night its streetlights glow in a row, looking just like a necklace wrapped around the water. That's why people call this road the 'Queen's Necklace.'

📍 Elephanta Caves

Cave temples on a small island you can only reach by boat. People carved them straight out of solid rock more than a thousand years ago. Inside wait huge stone sculptures of the Hindu god Shiva.

📍 Film City (Bollywood Studios)

A huge studio lot where Bollywood's singing, dancing movies come to life. More films are born here every year than in any other country. If you're lucky, you might even spot a scene being filmed!
