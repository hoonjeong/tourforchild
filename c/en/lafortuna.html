🌋 La Fortuna

The lucky town beside a volcano that slept for 400 years, then lit up the night sky for 42

📖 The Story

For about 400 years, a tall, perfectly cone-shaped mountain in Costa Rica just sat there, green and quiet, covered in rainforest. People who lived nearby farmed its slopes and never gave it a second thought. Nobody imagined that underneath all that green, the mountain was actually a sleeping volcano named Arenal.

Then, on one ordinary morning in 1968, without any warning at all, Arenal woke up — and erupted. Ash and rock came pouring out, and everyone nearby had to run. But an amazing stroke of luck happened too: the lava flowed away from the little village close by, sparing it completely. Grateful and relieved, the villagers renamed their town 'La Fortuna' — meaning 'the fortune,' or 'the luck.'

For the next 42 years, Arenal stayed wide awake, glowing with rivers of bright orange lava almost every single night. People traveled from all over the world just to sit in the dark and watch the mountain glow like a slow-motion fireworks show. The volcano even heated up underground water, so warm natural hot springs bubbled up nearby for everyone to soak in.

Arenal has been quietly resting again since 2010, but the rainforest around it is wide awake with life. Sloths hang lazily from branches, moving so slowly they seem to be dreaming. Wooden bridges let you walk high above the treetops, and a 70-meter waterfall crashes down into a pool so clear and blue, you'll want to jump right in.

📍 Places to visit

📍 Arenal Volcano

This near-perfect cone slept quietly for 400 years before erupting in 1968, then glowed with lava almost every night for 42 years. It's been resting again since 2010, but still looks ready to wake up.

📍 La Fortuna Waterfall

Hike down a jungle trail to reach this 70-meter waterfall crashing into a clear blue pool — cool off with a swim right where the spray meets the water.

📍 Arenal Hanging Bridges

These bridges let you walk high above the rainforest floor, right through the treetop canopy where monkeys swing and colorful birds dart between branches.

📍 Tabacon Hot Springs

These natural hot springs are warmed by the very volcano next door — no machines needed. Warm river water winds through the rainforest, perfect for soaking after a day of adventure.
