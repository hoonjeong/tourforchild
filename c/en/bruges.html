🇧🇪 Belgium

🦢 Bruges

So many bridges it's named after them—the medieval town where time seemed to stop

📖 The Story

Long, long ago, on the flat green plains of Belgium, a small town grew up where a river curved and looped. So many little bridges crossed its waterways that people started calling it 'Bruges'—bridges town. Merchants sailed in from all over Europe to trade wool, cloth, and spices, and Bruges quickly became one of the richest, busiest cities in the world.

Boats glided right along the canals into the heart of town, which is why people call Bruges 'the Venice of the North' today. But then something strange began to happen. The river connecting Bruges to the sea slowly filled up with sand. Big ships could no longer reach the harbor, so one by one, merchants sailed off to other cities instead.

That didn't mean Bruges disappeared—it just grew very quiet. For centuries, hardly anyone built anything new, so the crooked, cozy medieval houses, cobblestone streets, and towering bell tower stayed exactly as they were, like a city under a spell, fast asleep.

Then, about 150 years ago, travelers discovered this 'sleeping beauty' of a town and fell in love with it all over again. Today, when you cross Bruges's little bridges and follow canals where swans glide by, you're standing in one of the best-preserved medieval cities in Europe.

🤩 Did you know?

  • 1The name 'Bruges' means 'bridges'—and this small city really does have more than 50 of them!
  • 2Bruges's Belfry tower has 366 steps all the way to the top—and because it's so old, it leans just a little!
  • 3The Basilica of the Holy Blood still keeps a special relic said to have been brought back from a Crusade, long, long ago.
  • 4There are always swans gliding on the canals of Bruges—the city has kept a centuries-old tradition of caring for them, no matter what.
  • 5At a spot called Minnewater—'the Lake of Love'—legend says a kiss on its bridge makes love last forever.
  • 6Bruges has so many chocolate shops that, for its size, it's one of the most chocolate-packed cities on Earth!

📍 Places to visit

📍 The Markt & Belfry Tower

This is Bruges's beating heart—a square ringed by colorful old guild houses. Climb the 366 steps of the Belfry, and you can look out over the whole city from the top.

📍 Basilica of the Holy Blood

Small but dazzling, this chapel holds a special relic said to have traveled here from a Crusade long ago. Step inside the golden, glittering chapel, and you'll feel like you've traveled back in time.

📍 Rozenhoedkaai (Canal Viewpoint)

This is Bruges's most photographed canal view. Swans glide gracefully across the water, framed by old houses that look straight out of a postcard.

📍 Minnewater (Lake of Love)

Legend says a kiss on the bridge over this quiet lake makes love last forever. With swans drifting by, it's one of the most romantic spots in all of Bruges.
