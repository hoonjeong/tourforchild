⛄ Harbin

The Ice City where whole castles of ice glow rainbow bright at night

📖 The Story

Far in the northeast corner of China sits a city called Harbin. Winters here are so cold that the temperature can drop below -20°C, and the wide Songhua River that runs through town freezes rock solid—solid enough that people can walk right across it.

But the people of Harbin turned that bone-chilling cold into something wonderful. Every winter, workers cut huge blocks of ice straight out of the frozen river. Then they stack the blocks and carve them into castles, palaces, and even slides. Running since 1985, the Harbin International Ice and Snow Festival is still one of the biggest ice festivals on Earth.

When night falls, real magic happens. Colorful lights glow from inside the ice buildings, and the whole city turns into a sparkling, rainbow-colored wonderland. Kids can even zoom down slides carved entirely out of ice.

Harbin holds another secret too. Over a hundred years ago, a railway built by Russians passed through the city, leaving behind an onion-domed Saint Sophia Cathedral and Central Street, lined with European-style buildings. Between the fierce cold and this faraway charm, Harbin is a winter city you'll never forget once you've seen it.

📍 Places to visit

📍 Harbin Ice and Snow World

A giant winter theme park built entirely from ice cut out of the Songhua River. Castles, slides, and towers are all carved from solid ice. When night falls, colorful lights turn the whole place into a glowing ice kingdom.

📍 Saint Sophia Cathedral

An onion-domed church built by Russians over a century ago. It's no longer used for services—now it's Harbin's beloved landmark where photographers and travelers gather. The red-brick building looks especially beautiful under a snowy sky.

📍 Central Street (Zhongyang Dajie)

A century-old street lined with European-style buildings. Walking on its bumpy stone-block pavement feels like stepping into a European town. Bakeries along the street sell Russian-style bread and ice cream—even in freezing weather!

📍 Siberian Tiger Park

One of the world's largest breeding centers for endangered Siberian tigers. Here you can watch these huge striped cats prowl through wide, snow-covered enclosures up close.
