🗼 Paris

The 'ugly' iron tower Paris almost tore down — now the most loved skyline on Earth

📖 The Story

Long ago, Paris was already a beautiful city sitting along the curvy Seine River. People began stacking stones one by one to build a giant cathedral called Notre-Dame, and it took nearly 200 years to finish. Even today, dragon-like carvings called gargoyles perch on top, watching over the city.

Then, in 1889, Paris was getting ready for a huge World's Fair and decided to build something nobody had ever seen: a soaring iron tower over 300 meters tall, taller than anything on Earth. Thousands of iron pieces were bolted together like one enormous puzzle to raise it.

But here's the twist: hardly anyone in Paris liked it at first! Famous writers and artists signed an angry letter calling it an 'ugly iron skeleton,' and the plan was to tear it down after just 20 years. It was saved only when engineers realized its tall antenna was perfect for sending radio signals around the world.

Today, that 'ugly skeleton'—the Eiffel Tower—is one of the most loved towers on the planet. Every night it twinkles with thousands of lights, and if you ride to the top, all of Paris spreads out below you like a golden storybook map.

🤩 Did you know?

  • 1The Eiffel Tower is built from about 18,000 iron pieces held together with roughly 2.5 million rivets.
  • 2It was the tallest man-made structure in the world for 41 years, until 1930.
  • 3The tower grows a little taller in summer! Heat makes the iron expand, adding up to 15 cm to its height.
  • 4Engineer Gustave Eiffel kept a cozy secret apartment near the top, where he hosted guests like inventor Thomas Edison.
  • 5Notre-Dame Cathedral took almost 200 years to build and has monster carvings called gargoyles that double as rain spouts.
  • 6The Louvre Museum's famous glass pyramid isn't made of 666 panes like the old rumor says—it's actually 673.

📍 Places to visit

📍 Eiffel Tower

This is the very tower from the story. It was once hated, but now it's the symbol of Paris. Ride up to the top viewing deck and the whole city unfolds below you.

📍 Notre-Dame Cathedral

A cathedral built stone by stone over nearly 200 years. A huge fire badly damaged it in 2019, but people worked together to bring it back to its beautiful self.

📍 The Louvre Museum

One of the most famous museums on Earth, guarded by a giant glass pyramid at its entrance. People line up from all over the world just to see the tiny painting called the 'Mona Lisa.'

📍 Arc de Triomphe

A giant stone arch built by Napoleon to celebrate victory. The grand Champs-Élysées avenue stretches out straight from it, and it's considered Paris's most stylish street to walk.
