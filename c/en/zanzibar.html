🏝️ Zanzibar

The Spice Island where turquoise waves meet cinnamon-scented streets

📖 The Story

Off the coast of East Africa, in the warm blue Indian Ocean, floats an island so fragrant that sailors could smell it before they ever saw land. This is Zanzibar—a small archipelago with white sand beaches, a coral-pink sunset, and wooden sailboats called dhows gliding across the water just as they have for centuries.

Hundreds of years ago, traders from Arabia, Persia, and India sailed here chasing a treasure more valuable than gold: spices. Cloves, cinnamon, nutmeg, and black pepper grew so well on Zanzibar's rich soil that ships carried them across the world, and the island earned a nickname everyone still uses today—the Spice Island.

All those visitors didn't just trade goods and leave—they stayed, married, and built a new kind of city called Stone Town, where African, Arab, Persian, and Indian ideas mixed into one lively culture. Even the doors here are special, carved with flowers and studded with brass spikes, and no two look exactly alike. A boy born in one of Stone Town's little houses grew up to become Freddie Mercury, the singer who rocked stadiums around the world with his band Queen.

But Zanzibar's wonders aren't only in its towns. In Jozani Forest live red colobus monkeys found nowhere else on Earth, with fluffy white mohawks and orange-red fur. And on tiny Prison Island, giant tortoises that came from another island long ago are still slowly wandering around today—some of them older than your great-great-grandparents! Zanzibar is a small island bursting with big surprises.

📍 Places to visit

📍 Stone Town

This historic port city is the heart of Zanzibar, its narrow winding streets lined with carved wooden doors and balconies. It grew rich from centuries of spice trading, and singer Freddie Mercury was born in one of its old houses.

📍 Jozani Forest

This lush forest is the only place on Earth where wild red colobus monkeys live, swinging through the trees with their fluffy white mohawks. Walking its boardwalk trails feels like stepping into a real-life jungle storybook.

📍 Prison Island

A short boat ride from Stone Town, this tiny island is home to giant tortoises brought from the Seychelles long ago. Some of these gentle giants are over a hundred years old and love having their necks scratched!

📍 Kizimbani Spice Farm

On these fragrant farms you can rub, sniff, and taste the cloves, cinnamon, nutmeg, and vanilla that made Zanzibar famous. Guides love to challenge kids to guess each spice just by its smell!
