🎬 Wellington

The world's windiest capital, where movie magic and dinosaur-age creatures come alive

📖 The Story

Long, long ago, a great Polynesian explorer named Kupe sailed across the wide Pacific Ocean and found a beautiful harbour tucked at the tip of a brand-new land. He named it Te Whanganui-a-Tara, 'the great harbour of Tara.' Today, a small, colorful city wraps around that same harbour — Wellington, the capital of New Zealand, perched right where the North Island narrows to a point.

Wellington has a nickname nobody forgets: 'Windy Welly.' Wind rushing through a narrow strait nearby gets squeezed between two mountain ranges, so it comes roaring straight into the city. Houses here are painted every color and packed onto steep green hillsides, and a little red cable car climbs through a tunnel so people don't have to walk all the way up.

Wellington has another nickname too: 'Wellywood.' Long ago, filmmakers here built incredible workshops to dream up dragons, elves, and hobbits for movies like The Lord of the Rings. Even now, giant models and magical costumes are born in Wellington's workshops before they ever appear on movie screens around the world.

Best of all, right in the middle of the city sits a magical valley called Zealandia. Long ago, hungry animals that arrived from other countries gobbled up New Zealand's rare birds, so people built a giant fence with no gaps at all, sealing out every unwanted visitor. Now kiwi birds sleep safely at night, and tuatara — reptiles older than the dinosaurs — bask in the sun just as they did millions of years ago.

🤩 Did you know?

  • 1Wellington is one of the windiest cities on Earth. Locals call it 'Windy Welly,' and sometimes the wind is so strong it can nearly blow you off your feet!
  • 2Wellington is the southernmost capital city of any country in the whole world.
  • 3The movie wizards at Weta Workshop built the monsters, armor, and magical worlds for The Lord of the Rings, The Hobbit, and King Kong — all right here in Wellington.
  • 4Zealandia is wrapped in a giant fence with absolutely no gaps — the first sanctuary like it ever built — keeping hungry pests out so native birds can thrive again.
  • 5Tuatara, reptiles that live at Zealandia, are relatives of animals that lived alongside the dinosaurs more than 200 million years ago.
  • 6Wellington's little red cable car has been climbing the hill since 1902, and sometimes tiny blue penguins waddle ashore in the harbour at night!

📍 Places to visit

📍 Zealandia Ecosanctuary

A magical nature reserve right in the middle of the city. Thanks to its gapless fence, invading animals can't get in, so you can meet endangered kiwi birds and tuatara that have lived here since the age of dinosaurs, up close.

📍 Wellington Cable Car

A little red train that has been climbing the hill since 1902. Ride through the tunnel to the top and Wellington's harbour and colorful rooftops spread out below you.

📍 Weta Workshop

The movie workshop that created the magical worlds of The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit. You can see giant monster models and armor up close — that's how Wellington earned the nickname 'Wellywood.'

📍 Te Papa Tongarewa Museum

A giant museum that packs in all of New Zealand. You can discover Maori stories and culture, a shaking volcano model, and even a giant squid — an exciting place to explore.
