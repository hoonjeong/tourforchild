🏗️ Dubai

The tiny fishing village on the sand that grew into a city of record-breaking dreams

📖 The Story

Not long ago—less than a hundred years back—Dubai was just a small, sandy town beside a saltwater creek. Fishermen sailed out each morning, and brave divers plunged deep underwater holding their breath, searching for pearls hidden inside oyster shells. Camel caravans crossed the desert bringing spices and cloth to trade, and life moved as slowly as the tide.

Then something changed everything: oil was found beneath the desert, and the rulers of Dubai made a bold decision. Instead of keeping all that treasure buried, they used it to build roads, schools, an airport, and a busy port—turning a sleepy town into a city buzzing with cranes and construction almost overnight.

Dubai didn't just want to grow—it wanted to break records. It built the tallest building anyone had ever made, so tall that clouds sometimes drift past its windows. It even carved a giant palm-tree-shaped island out of the sea, adding kilometers of brand-new beach to the coastline. People all over the world looked at photos from space and could hardly believe it was real.

But Dubai never forgot where it came from. Just minutes from the shiny skyscrapers, wooden boats called abras still putter across the same creek, carrying passengers the way they did a hundred years ago. Walk from the golden towers to the old spice market, and you'll feel like you've stepped through two different centuries in one afternoon.

📍 Places to visit

📍 Burj Khalifa

The tallest building in the world, reaching over 828 meters into the sky. Ride the elevator up and you might see clouds floating below you. At night, it lights up with dazzling shows of color and fireworks.

📍 Palm Jumeirah

A whole island shaped like a giant palm tree, built by piling sand into the sea. Its "trunk" and "fronds" hold homes, hotels, and beaches that didn't exist a few decades ago.

📍 Al Fahidi Historic District & Dubai Creek

Wander narrow lanes of old wind-tower houses, then hop on a wooden abra boat to cross the creek exactly like traders did a hundred years ago. It's the best place to feel Dubai's story before the skyscrapers.

📍 Burj Al Arab

A hotel shaped like a billowing sail, built on its own man-made island just off the beach. It was once the tallest hotel in the world and is one of the most photographed buildings on Earth.
