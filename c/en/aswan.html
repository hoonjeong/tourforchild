⛵ Aswan

Egypt's colorful southern gateway, where a giant unfinished obelisk still sleeps in stone

📖 The Story

Long, long ago, Aswan sat at Egypt's southern gate, where the Nile squeezes between rocky islands. Ancient Egyptians came here to quarry pink granite, the strong, sparkly stone kings used to build giant statues and towering obelisks all over Egypt.

One day, workers began carving the biggest obelisk ever attempted, right out of the bedrock. It would have stood taller than a ten-story building! But partway through, a crack split the stone, and the workers had to stop. That giant unfinished obelisk still lies there today, half-carved into the ground.

Aswan also holds a temple to the goddess Isis, built on a little island called Philae. When engineers built the huge Aswan High Dam nearby, they worried the rising water would swallow the temple whole. So people from all over the world teamed up, cut the entire temple into thousands of numbered blocks, and rebuilt it—stone by stone—on a higher island, safe and dry.

Today Aswan is full of color: Nubian villages painted in bright blues and yellows line the Nile, feluccas (traditional sailboats) glide past golden dunes, and camels rest near ancient temples. It's one of Egypt's most beautiful, laid-back cities.

🤩 Did you know?

  • 1If finished, the Unfinished Obelisk would have been the largest obelisk ever carved—about 42 meters long, and as heavy as 200 elephants!
  • 2Ancient workers had no metal tools strong enough to cut granite, so they pounded it for months using hard, round stones called dolerite.
  • 3To save Philae Temple from flooding, workers cut it into more than 40,000 blocks and rebuilt it piece by piece on a higher island.
  • 4The Aswan High Dam created Lake Nasser, one of the largest man-made lakes on Earth.
  • 5Long ago, a scientist named Eratosthenes used the sunlight shining straight down a well in Aswan to figure out the size of the entire Earth—without ever leaving the ground!
  • 6Nubian villages near Aswan paint their houses in bright blues, pinks, and yellows—some families even keep a pet crocodile out front for good luck!

📍 Places to visit

📍 The Unfinished Obelisk

A massive obelisk still lies half-carved into the bedrock, cracked before workers could finish it. If completed, it would have been the tallest obelisk Egypt ever made. You can walk right up and touch the ancient tool marks!

📍 Philae Temple

A beautiful temple built for the goddess Isis, rescued piece by piece and rebuilt on a higher island to escape the rising water. Sailing there by small boat feels like floating into a storybook.

📍 Aswan High Dam

A giant wall across the Nile that tamed the river's floods and created Lake Nasser, one of the biggest lakes people ever built. Standing on top, you can see the calm blue lake stretching toward the horizon.

📍 Nubian Village

Colorful Nubian houses painted blue, pink, and yellow line the Nile's banks near Elephantine Island. Visitors ride camels or sailboats to reach them, then sip sweet hibiscus tea while camels nap in the shade.
