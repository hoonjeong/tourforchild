🎓 Oxford

The dreaming-spire town where wizards, hobbits, and Alice in Wonderland were all born

📖 The Story

Picture a small English town where every street seems to have its own castle. That's Oxford — a city built almost entirely around one giant, ancient university. Its golden stone towers and spires poke into the sky so beautifully that people call it the 'city of dreaming spires.' Students have been studying here for over 900 years.

Nobody knows the exact day Oxford's university began, but people were already teaching here by the year 1096 — before England even had its famous King Richard the Lionheart! Over time, the university grew into 39 separate colleges, each one like its own tiny castle with its own dining hall, chapel, and garden. Walking through Oxford feels like wandering through dozens of hidden kingdoms tucked inside one city.

Oxford has sprinkled magic into stories for hundreds of years. One sunny afternoon, a math professor named Charles took a little girl named Alice rowing down the river and made up a story to keep her entertained — that story became 'Alice in Wonderland'! Decades later, two professor friends met almost every week in a cozy pub to read each other tales about hobbits and talking lions — and that's how 'The Lord of the Rings' and 'Narnia' came to life.

Today you can still walk into the very dining hall that helped inspire Hogwarts in the Harry Potter movies, climb the spiral stairs of a library older than most countries, and hop in a flat wooden boat called a punt to float lazily down the river — just like Alice did. Oxford isn't just a university town. It's a real place where imaginary worlds began.

📍 Places to visit

📍 Bodleian Library

One of the oldest libraries in Europe, and by law it gets a free copy of nearly every book published in the UK. Wander its ancient reading rooms and you're standing among millions of books, some guarded here for over 400 years.

📍 Radcliffe Camera

This round, golden-domed building is Oxford's most famous postcard view. It's actually a reading room for the Bodleian Library, and its beautiful curved shape has appeared in movies as a stand-in for magical libraries.

📍 Christ Church College

Its grand dining hall, lined with paintings and long wooden tables, helped inspire Hogwarts' Great Hall in the Harry Potter films. It's also where Alice Liddell — the real girl behind Alice in Wonderland — grew up, since her father ran the college.

📍 The Eagle and Child

A cozy little pub where writer friends J.R.R. Tolkien and C.S. Lewis met almost every week to read aloud their new stories. Sitting in their favorite back room, you can imagine hobbits and talking lions coming to life for the very first time.
