🐠 Cairns

Where the world's biggest reef meets one of its oldest jungles

📖 The Story

At the very top of Australia sits a town called Cairns, where something amazing happens. On one side, a bright blue ocean sparkles. On the other, a deep green jungle grows thick and wild. And here's the best part—they're so close together that you could swim in the sea in the morning and walk through the rainforest by afternoon.

Just off the coast of Cairns lies the Great Barrier Reef, the biggest coral reef on Earth. It stretches over 2,300 kilometers—so long you can even spot it from outer space! This giant reef wasn't built in a day. Tiny creatures called coral polyps built it up, piece by piece, over thousands and thousands of years. Now it's home to sea turtles, sharks, and rainbow-colored fish just like Nemo.

Behind Cairns, mountains rise up covered in a rainforest called the Daintree. It's about 180 million years old—older than the Amazon rainforest! Giant birds called cassowaries, with bright blue necks, stomp through these ancient trees. And Aboriginal peoples have lived here for more than 50,000 years, learning every path, plant, and secret of this forest.

That's what makes Cairns one of a kind. It's the only place on Earth where the world's biggest reef and one of its oldest rainforests sit right next to each other. Swim with a turtle in the morning, then walk beneath trees that dinosaurs once nibbled on by afternoon—only in Cairns!

🤩 Did you know?

  • 1The Great Barrier Reef is the largest living structure on Earth—built by tiny coral polyps—and it's so big you can spot it from space!
  • 2The Daintree Rainforest is about 180 million years old, making it older than the Amazon rainforest.
  • 3The cassowary, a big blue-necked bird, can't fly but can run super fast. People call it 'the world's most dangerous bird,' but really it's shy—and it helps grow the forest by eating fruit and spreading the seeds.
  • 4Cairns has a giant free public swimming pool called the Esplanade Lagoon, built because the ocean has stinging jellyfish in summer.
  • 5The old Kuranda train track was carved through the mountains by hand more than 100 years ago, passing 15 tunnels and a waterfall—while the Skyrail cable car floats you right above the rainforest treetops.
  • 6Aboriginal peoples have lived among this reef and rainforest for more than 50,000 years—one of the oldest continuous cultures on Earth.

📍 Places to visit

📍 Green Island

A tiny coral-reef island just a 40-minute boat ride from Cairns. The whole island grew right on top of the reef, so you can snorkel straight off the beach and meet colorful fish and sea turtles.

📍 Cape Tribulation

Here the ancient Daintree Rainforest grows right down to the sand—the only place on Earth where two World Heritage wonders, rainforest and reef, touch. Walk through jungle, and in just a few steps you're on a white sandy beach.

📍 Kuranda Scenic Railway & Skyrail

Ride a train carved by hand through the mountains more than 100 years ago, crossing waterfalls and bridges up to the jungle village of Kuranda. Come back down floating above the rainforest canopy on the Skyrail cable car.

📍 Cairns Esplanade Lagoon

A huge free swimming lagoon right by the sea in Cairns. In summer, stinging jellyfish can show up in the ocean, so everyone swims here instead, safe and cool. The grassy lawns under the palm trees are perfect for a picnic too.
