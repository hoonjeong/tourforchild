🔵 Chefchaouen

Morocco's 'Blue Pearl', where an entire town is painted the color of the sky

📖 The Story

Tucked into Morocco's rugged Rif Mountains sits a truly one-of-a-kind town called Chefchaouen. It began in 1471, when a local leader built a mountain fortress to guard against invaders arriving from across the sea. Two nearby peaks look like a pair of horns, which is exactly what the name Chefchaouen means!

Not long after, people fleeing Spain found safety in this hidden mountain town. For centuries afterward, Chefchaouen became a secretive place—outsiders of other faiths simply weren't allowed inside. In fact, the very first European to enter did so only about 140 years ago, and even then, in disguise!

So why is the whole town painted blue? One story says the refugees who settled here began painting their homes a blue that reminded them of the sky and the heavens above. People also noticed that blue paint kept houses cooler in summer and seemed to keep pesky mosquitoes away. House by house, the color spread until, like magic, the entire town turned blue.

Walk Chefchaouen's narrow lanes today and you'll find walls, staircases, and doorframes all rippling in shades of blue. Sleepy cats nap in the cool alleys, and clear mountain water still trickles from a spring at the edge of town. It feels like wandering through a piece of the sky—there's truly no other place like it.

📍 Places to visit

📍 The Medina (Blue Old Town)

A maze of narrow, winding lanes where the walls, stairs, and doorframes are painted every shade of blue. Wandering through feels like swimming inside a painting.

📍 Plaza Uta el-Hammam & the Kasbah

The town's main square, home to the Kasbah fortress built way back in 1471. Today it holds a garden and a small museum, so you can wander its old walls and feel where the whole town began.

📍 Ras el-Maa Spring

At the edge of the medina, clear mountain water tumbles down like a little waterfall. For generations, villagers came here to wash clothes and fetch water—you can still hear the cool splash today.

📍 The Spanish Mosque Viewpoint

An old hilltop mosque with a sweeping view over the whole blue town below. At sunset, the town glows pink and blue together, looking just like a painting.
