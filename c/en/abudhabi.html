🕌 Abu Dhabi

A tiny desert island that a gazelle's footprints turned into a shining modern city

📖 The Story

Long, long ago, a hunter chased a leaping gazelle onto a small, sandy island off Arabia's coast. There, hidden in the dry ground, he found something amazing—fresh, sweet water bubbling up in the middle of the desert! People named the island 'Abu Dhabi,' which means 'father of the gazelle,' and families from the Bani Yas tribe moved there to live by that precious water.

For hundreds of years, life on the island stayed small and simple. Brave pearl divers held their breath and dove deep into the warm Gulf sea without any oxygen tanks, searching for shiny pearls hidden inside shells. Up on the sand, hunters trained falcons to swoop and soar beside them—birds so trusted, they became like part of the family.

Then, everything changed almost overnight. In the late 1950s, workers discovered something huge hiding under the desert and the sea nearby: oil. Almost like magic, the quiet fishing town began growing taller and shinier every year, turning into one of the most modern cities on Earth.

But Abu Dhabi never forgot where it came from. Today it has the world's biggest hand-knotted carpet inside a giant mosque glowing white, a museum with a dome that sprinkles 'rain of light' onto visitors below, and falcons that fly on airplanes with their very own passports. From a gazelle's footprints to shooting stars of light—what a journey!

📍 Places to visit

📍 Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque

This gleaming white mosque is so big it can hold 41,000 worshippers at once. Inside lies the world's largest hand-knotted carpet, and at night, giant chandeliers sparkle like a sky full of crystal stars.

📍 Qasr Al Hosn

This is Abu Dhabi's oldest building, built around the very freshwater well that gave the city its start. Once a fort and a royal home, it now tells the story of how a tiny desert village grew into a great city.

📍 Louvre Abu Dhabi

A giant silver dome floats above this museum, punched with 7,850 star-shaped holes. When the sun shines through, it feels like walking under a sparkling 'rain of light.'

📍 Ferrari World, Yas Island

Under the world's biggest Ferrari-shaped roof, thrill-seekers ride the Formula Rossa—the fastest roller coaster on the planet. It rockets riders to 240 km/h faster than a race car can!
