🏜️ Doha

From a pearl-diving fishing village to a desert city of glittering towers

📖 The Story

Not that long ago, Doha was a small fishing village hugging the edge of the Persian Gulf. Before there were skyscrapers, there were pearl divers — men who took one huge breath, clipped their nose shut, and swam down into the warm sea to find oysters hiding pearls. It was hard, brave work, and for centuries it was how Doha made its living.

Then everything changed. Deep under the desert and the sea nearby, people discovered one of the largest treasures on Earth: natural gas. Almost overnight, tiny Doha grew rich, and glittering glass towers began rising along the curved waterfront called the Corniche, shaped like a string of pearls itself.

But Doha never forgot where it came from. At Souq Waqif, an old market rebuilt brick by brick, you can still smell spices, watch falcons perched proudly for sale, and imagine pearl traders haggling the same way people did generations ago. The Museum of Islamic Art, floating on its own little island, guards treasures from three continents inside a building shaped like a fortress.

Head out past the city and the wonders keep coming. In the golden desert south of Doha, sand dunes tumble right into the sea at a place called the Inland Sea, and some dunes even 'sing' as sand slides down them. In 2022, this small pearl-diving country became the first Arab nation ever to host the World Cup, with the whole world watching.

🤩 Did you know?

  • 1Pearl divers in old Doha wore only a nose clip made of turtle shell or bone, holding their breath and diving over 30 meters down more than 40 times a day.
  • 2Qatar sits atop one of the largest natural-gas fields on the planet, one that stretches under the seafloor and connects to neighboring Iran.
  • 3The Museum of Islamic Art was designed by the same architect behind the Louvre's glass pyramid in Paris — and a whole man-made island was built just for the museum.
  • 4At Souq Waqif's falcon market, hooded falcons sit quietly on padded perches waiting to be sold — some cost more than a car!
  • 5South of the city, desert meets sea at the Inland Sea, where giant sand dunes are said to make a real booming, singing sound when they come crashing down.
  • 6In 2022, Doha hosted the FIFA World Cup — the first time it was ever held in the Middle East, with brand-new, air-conditioned stadiums built just for the games.

📍 Places to visit

📍 Museum of Islamic Art

This museum stands like a stone fortress on its own tiny island. It was designed by the same architect behind the Louvre's glass pyramid, and inside are dazzling Islamic art treasures over a thousand years old.

📍 Souq Waqif

A centuries-old market rebuilt exactly as it once looked, selling spices, fabric, and even falcons. Come evening, whole families stroll its lively lanes — it's the beating heart of old Doha.

📍 Katara Cultural Village

A seaside village with a traditional pearling boat and an open-air amphitheater. Walk along its beach and you can almost hear stories of the days when Doha was famous for pearls.

📍 Inland Sea (Khor Al Adaid)

A magical spot where golden desert dunes tumble straight into the sea. This UNESCO-recognized desert wonder even 'sings' when its sand comes crashing down.
